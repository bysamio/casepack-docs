title: "Admin: Customers"
description: Manage MSP customer records and their associated tenants.
---

The Customers page lets Super Admins manage customer records — the organizations your MSP serves. Customers can be linked to one or more tenants for organizational clarity.

## Overview

- Accessible to **Super Admin** account role only
- Found in the **Customers** sidebar item (under Administration)
- Customers are organizational labels — they group tenants by client

## Customer List

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Name** | Customer display name |
| **Tenants** | Number of tenants linked to this customer |
| **Created** | When the customer record was created |

## Creating a Customer

1. Navigate to **Customers**
2. Click **"Create Customer"**
3. Enter the customer **Name**
4. Click **"Create"**

## Customer Detail

Click a customer to view:

- **Name** — Editable display name
- **Linked tenants** — List of tenants assigned to this customer
- **Delete** — Remove the customer record

> Deleting a customer does not delete linked tenants — they become unassigned.

## Linking Tenants to Customers

Tenants are linked to customers from the [Tenants](/admin-tenants/) page:

1. Navigate to **Tenants**
2. Edit a tenant
3. Select or change the assigned customer

## Tips & Best Practices

- Create a customer record for each MSP client
- Link all customer tenants for a clear organizational view
- Use consistent naming (match your PSA tool's customer names)
- Customer records are lightweight — create them freely for organizational clarity
